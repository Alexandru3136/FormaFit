# Forma Product Brief

Forma is a mobile-first web app for nutrition, workouts, progress tracking, and AI-assisted coaching.

## Promise

Forma helps people decide what to eat, how to train, and how to stay consistent without shame or unnecessary complexity.

## MVP Scope

- User accounts and secure sessions.
- Onboarding for body metrics, goals, activity, food preferences, and training availability.
- Calorie and macro calculator.
- Today dashboard with calories, protein, training, and coach guidance.
- Food journal via natural-language text.
- AI meal ideas from ingredients and user goals.
- Basic workout plans: full-body, upper/lower, and muscle-group splits.
- Exercise library with instructions, common mistakes, alternatives, and visual references.
- AI coach through OmniRoute, called only from the server.
- Free and Premium boundaries prepared in the product model.

## Later Scope

- Food photo calorie estimation.
- Shopping lists and meal prep.
- Restaurant mode.
- Push notifications and subscription reminders.
- Stripe subscriptions.
- Admin console for users, billing, AI usage, content, and audit logs.
- PWA installability for Android and iOS.

## Product Principles

- Mobile-first and PWA-ready.
- Helpful, calm tone. No shame-based fitness messaging.
- Estimates must be labeled as estimates.
- Sensitive access decisions happen on the server.
- Code should be modular, typed, validated, and readable by future maintainers.
